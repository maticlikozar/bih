The journey

Eight days
on two wheels.

Each day has a different personality — from river valleys and mountain roads to Ottoman towns and the heat of southern Herzegovina.

DAY01

Kranj → Vinica → Plitvice

Vinica Plitvice Lakes

Morning — Kranj

Start in Kranj and ride south toward Vinica before continuing into Croatia and on to Plitvice Lakes.

🏞️ VINICA & PLITVICE

Vinica makes a good first stop before crossing into Croatia. Finish the day at Plitvice Lakes, one of the region's most spectacular natural sights.

🏨 Overnight: Plitvice

DAY02

Plitvice → Željava → Bihać

Željava Air Base Una River Bihać

Morning — Plitvice Lakes

Start early and visit Plitvice Lakes before the biggest crowds and summer heat.

✈️ ŽELJAVA UNDERGROUND AIRBASE

Ride to the abandoned Željava underground airbase, one of the Balkans' most unusual Cold War sites. Explore only accessible, established areas.

⚠️ The wider region has a history of wartime mine/UXO contamination. Stay on established access routes and never enter unknown tunnels or terrain.

🌊 UNA RIVER & BIHAĆ

Finish the day beside the Una River and take some time to explore Bihać's historic centre and riverside.

Afternoon — Bihać

Cross into Bosnia, settle beside the Una River and enjoy a relaxed dinner in town.

🏨 Overnight: Bihać

DAY03

Bihać → Štrbački Buk → Kulen Vakuf → Martin Brod → Jajce

Štrbački Buk Kulen Vakuf Martin Brod Jajce

💦 ŠTRBAČKI BUK

Stop at Štrbački Buk, one of the most impressive waterfalls on the Una River, surrounded by forest and dramatic river scenery.

🏞️ KULEN VAKUF & MARTIN BROD

Continue through the Una valley via Kulen Vakuf and Martin Brod, with plenty of opportunities for riverside views and short breaks.

Una National Park

The first major motorcycle riding day. Follow the Una valley through forests, mountains and brilliant turquoise water.

Evening — Jajce

See the Jajce Waterfall, medieval fortress and Old Town. If daylight allows, continue to Pliva Lakes and the historic wooden watermills.

🏨 Overnight: Jajce

DAY04

Jajce → Prozor → Rama Lake

Ramsko Jezero Šćit Monastery

🏔️ RAMSKO JEZERO

Ride toward Prozor and continue to spectacular Ramsko Jezero. Don't make it a roadside photo stop — spend the afternoon riding around the lake and enjoying the mountain scenery.

⛪ ŠĆIT FRANCISCAN MONASTERY

Visit the Franciscan monastery on the Šćit peninsula and enjoy the views across the lake.

Slow afternoon

Swim if conditions allow, relax by the water, take a short sunset ride and have dinner overlooking the lake.

🏨 Overnight: Rama Lake / Šćit
↗ Open Day 4 in Google Maps ↗ Open Day 4 in Maps
DAY05

Rama → Konjic → Igman → Sarajevo

Konjic Old Bridge Igman Olympic Resort Baščaršija

Morning — Neretva gateway

Leave Rama after breakfast and ride through Prozor and Jablanica before stopping in Konjic for coffee and the old Ottoman bridge.

🌉 KONJIC OLD BRIDGE

Stop in Konjic to see the historic Ottoman-era bridge and take a break before climbing toward Igman.

🏚️ Igman Olympic Resort

Continue toward Mount Igman and Malo Polje. The abandoned Hotel Igman / Olympic resort complex is associated with the 1984 Sarajevo Winter Olympics.

⚠️ Stay on established roads and known visitor areas. Do not explore random forest tracks, abandoned military positions or unmarked areas.

Evening — Sarajevo

Finish with traditional Sarajevo ćevapi and somun.

🏨 Overnight: Sarajevo

DAY06

Sarajevo → Neretva → Mostar

Neretva Valley Stari Most Mostar Old Town

The signature riding day

Leave Sarajevo in the morning and follow the road south through Konjic and Jablanica. The landscape becomes increasingly dramatic as you descend toward Herzegovina.

🌊 NERETVA VALLEY

Follow the Neretva south from Sarajevo through Konjic and Jablanica, with dramatic mountains rising on both sides of the valley.

🌉 STARI MOST — MOSTAR

Explore Mostar's historic centre and Stari Most, then walk through the old bazaar and along the Neretva. Sunset is particularly good from the Old Town.

Afternoon — Mostar

Check in, park the motorcycles and explore the city on foot. Walk through Stari Most, the Old Bazaar and the Neretva riverfront.

Best moment: See Stari Most around sunset — then again after dark.

🏨 Overnight: Mostar

DAY07

Mostar → Blagaj → Stolac → Počitelj → Kravica → Livno

Blagaj Tekija Stolac Počitelj Kravica Waterfalls

💧 BLAGAJ — BUNA SPRING & TEKIJA

Visit the powerful Buna River spring and the historic Blagaj Tekija, set dramatically beneath the cliffs.

Morning — Blagaj

Visit the Buna River spring, Blagaj Tekija and the riverside cafés.

💦 KRAVICA WATERFALLS

Finish the Herzegovina sightseeing section at Kravica, a spectacular series of waterfalls and a great place to cool down in summer.

Late morning — Kravica

Spend around 2–3 hours at the waterfalls. In August, this is the main swimming and cooling-off break.

🏰 STOLAC

Stop in Stolac and explore its historic surroundings before continuing toward the Neretva valley.

🏘️ POČITELJ

Walk through the historic Ottoman settlement of Počitelj and climb toward the fortress for views over the Neretva valley.

Afternoon — Stolac & Počitelj

Continue through Stolac before exploring the historic Ottoman settlement of Počitelj and climbing toward the fortress for views across the Neretva valley.

🏨 Overnight: Livno

DAY08

Livno → Grahovo → Drvar → Bihać → Plitvice → Metlika → Kranj

Bosansko Grahovo Drvar Plitvice Lakes Metlika Kranj

🏔️ BOSANSKO GRAHOVO & DRVAR

Ride through the open mountain landscapes around Bosansko Grahovo before continuing to Drvar and onward toward Bihać.

🌊 PLITVICE LAKES

Pass back through Plitvice on the return journey and enjoy a final stretch of the Croatian karst landscape before returning to Slovenia.

🇸🇮 METLIKA

Cross back into Slovenia via Metlika before making the final push toward Kranj.

The long final push

Ride through Bosansko Grahovo and Drvar to Bihać, pass through Plitvice, then cross back into Slovenia via Metlika before finishing in Kranj.

⚠️ Start early. Keep the stops flexible and check border conditions. If weather, fatigue or delays make the day too long, shorten the route rather than riding tired.
🏁 Finish: Kranj

Ride smart

Adventure needs
a little discipline.

  • Stay on established roads around former military and war areas.
  • Never enter unknown tunnels, buildings or forest tracks.
  • Take particular care around Željava and Igman.
  • Don't blindly follow GPS onto unpaved or restricted tracks.
  • Fuel whenever convenient rather than waiting for the next large town.
  • Carry plenty of water, especially in Herzegovina.
  • Check border crossing conditions before Day 7.
  • Summer thunderstorms can change mountain-road conditions quickly.
  • Allow extra time for villages, livestock, slow traffic and roadworks.
  • Most importantly: don't ride tired on the long final day.
